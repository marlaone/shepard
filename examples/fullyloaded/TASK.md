The Meier family wants to travel by car to visit relatives
in Portugal. The itinerary has been set, and
it will not be possible without overnight stays. The
Meiers have therefore looked at numerous hotels along
the route on the Internet and determined a
rating for each one.
Siblings Lara and Paul are allowed to choose
which hotels to stay at. In the Meiers' somewhat
old car, the two cannot charge their cell phones.
Therefore, they can drive for a maximum of six hours
per day. In total, the trip may take a maximum of five
days.
Under these conditions, Lara and Paul want to choose
the nicest hotels possible: the lowest rating of a hotel
in their selection should be as high as possible.
Task 2
Write a program that selects the hotels.
 Your program should read in the information about
the hotels, namely their locations along the
route and their ratings, and output a
selection according to Lara and Paul's wishes.

Apply your program to at least all
the examples you find on the BWINF website
and document the results.
